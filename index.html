BeatHaven Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

BeatHaven (com.beathaven.app) is provided by Konwars (Yağız Yılmaz) in Türkiye. For privacy questions or requests, contact beathaven.support@gmail.com. This policy explains how the app uses information for local music playback, optional Premium subscriptions and advertising for eligible Free users.

Information used on your device

BeatHaven does not require a BeatHaven account and does not operate a developer-hosted music, profile or cloud-sync service.

  • Music library: With your Android media permission, the app reads accessible local audio and metadata, including titles, artists, albums, duration, artwork, file names, locations and audio classification flags. It uses these to find, classify, display, search and play music. BeatHaven does not upload your audio library to our servers.
  • Favorites and playlists: Song references, playlist names, ordering information and related timestamps are stored in the app's local database.
  • Profile: A name you enter and a resized copy of a photo you select are stored in private app storage. The app accesses the selected image through Android's picker; it does not require access to your entire photo library for this feature.
  • Preferences and searches: Theme, accent, navigation preferences, playback options, equalizer settings and language choices are saved locally. Up to ten recent search phrases are kept locally; searches operate on your local library, not an online search service.
  • Playback: Queue, playback controls and sleep timer state support the running player. Track information and artwork are supplied to Android's media session and notifications and may appear on the lock screen or compatible connected media controls. BeatHaven does not maintain a separate listening-history analytics service.

Our code does not supply these local library, profile and search data to AdMob for advertising targeting.

Google Play purchases

The app includes Google Play Billing for optional Premium subscriptions, whose availability depends on Google Play product configuration. Premium removes advertising. A paid offer is made available only when it provides a genuine benefit to the user. Google Play handles payment and account processing; BeatHaven does not receive your payment card details. The app processes product identifiers, purchase tokens and purchase/subscription status to acknowledge purchases, restore access and determine Premium eligibility. It does not send these records to a developer-operated verification server.

A local timestamp of the last verified Premium entitlement can support access during temporary connectivity problems for up to 24 hours. This is an access-cache limit, not a promise that every associated record is deleted after 24 hours. Premium status is also checked through Google Play. Clearing app data does not delete Google's transaction records or cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in Google Play.

Advertising, age and privacy choices

Eligible Free users may receive Google AdMob banner and interstitial ads. Premium users do not receive ads. Ads are not requested for users under 13, users whose age is unknown, or users for whom age, regional or format eligibility has not been established. For ages 13–17, advertising may be enabled only with the required child/teen protections and non-personalized serving. Interstitials remain disabled where age-appropriate format and dismissal requirements cannot be satisfied.

The first-use age screen offers age bands under 13, 13–15, 16–17 and 18+. The chosen band is stored locally and can be changed in Settings; no exact birth date or identity document is requested. Declining to provide an age leaves advertising disabled. Age data is kept until changed or app data is cleared; it is not an advertising identifier.

Google's advertising SDK can process IP-derived approximate location, advertising/app identifiers, interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to applicable age restrictions. These are distinct from local music/profile data. Google UMP manages applicable advertising privacy choices. Its permission to request ads does not replace age eligibility. Where available, use Settings to revisit privacy choices; Android also provides advertising identifier controls. Ads SDK initialization must follow the applicable age/privacy checks. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Interstitials are considered only at suitable user transitions, not on app launch or during music playback. They are not shown in the first session, have at least a 15-minute interval, and are limited to one per session and two in a rolling 24 hours. Local display records enforce these limits. Recent display records are retained as needed for the rolling limit; the last-display time and session eligibility may remain until app data is cleared. An unavailable ad does not delay the user's action.

Other providers, security and transfers

The app has no separate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics integration. Google Play and Gmail processing are separate from this statement. Google processes data under its own terms and Privacy Policy, which also explains international processing, retention and privacy controls. We do not claim that Google-held information stays in your country or that we can directly erase it.

Local data uses Android app storage protections. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention, backup and your controls

App-managed persistent local data remains until you change or remove it through the available controls, clear app storage or uninstall. You can remove favorites/playlists and clear recent searches in the app. Reset Settings resets app preferences; it does not erase your profile, playlists, favorites or all other data. To remove the full local app data set, use Android's app-storage controls. This does not delete your original music files or source photo, nor does revoking media permission delete those files. Permission can be revoked in Android settings, limiting library access.

Current backup rules exclude the database, profile, app settings and Premium cache. Other preferences, such as recent searches, equalizer or language storage, may be included in Android backup/device transfer depending on your system and settings. Uninstalling does not necessarily remove an existing system backup; manage those backups through the relevant device/account controls.

Records held by Google, including purchase and support-service records follow Google's retention and privacy controls. Applicable law may provide rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data. Contact us about data we control; use Google's privacy tools for Google-held records. We cannot retrieve your private local app data remotely. If you email us, your email address and message are processed to answer you. Support email is handled through Gmail (Google). We retain support correspondence for no more than six months after the last message, then delete it. Yağız Yılmaz is responsible for applying this retention and deletion schedule; it is not an automated function of the Android app. Google may retain provider-side records under its own policies.

Children, updates and contact

BeatHaven is intended for ages 13–15, 16–17 and adults aged 18 and over, including secondary-school and university students and adult music listeners. It is not specifically directed at children under 13. This audience statement does not mean that all users aged 13 or over are adults or can independently consent to every form of data processing; applicable protections depend on age and location.

If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, contact us using the address below. We cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your child's device; the local-data controls described above remain available.
